A new chapter awaits

Careers now fund your path into authorship, with new skill progression, 18 upgrades, readership and optional paid editing. New books earn declining sales for two game years. Continuous writing and optional career and training automation are available.

Keep your progress to convert older upgrades into their replacement tiers, without cash refunds. Existing book income becomes a declining one-year sales tail. Your save is backed up before conversion. Money, experience and your current manuscript carry over, but income and upkeep will change.

Would you like to continue your journey or restart with the new balance? Restarting erases your current game progress.

The Golden Pen Awakens

On your 65th birthday, the strange pen you found long ago begins to glow. You realize it offers a chance to start over, wiser and more experienced.

By clicking the pen, you can retire and restart your career, losing all levels and money. However, you will gain experience multipliers for all jobs and skills, allowing you to progress much faster in your next life.

A Legendary Opportunity

At the grand age of 200, the Golden Pen hums with immense power. It offers you a path to true immortality, a chance to become a legend.

If you accept, all levels, money, and even your max level bonuses will be reset. You will be reborn as a fresh slate. However, you will unlock new skills and gain Fame, which will heavily impact all future lives and book sales.
